# Sunesis and Kumuluz

Sunesis develops and delivers Kumuluz. Kumuluz is its family of reusable enterprise platform products and accelerators; Sunesis provides the architecture, implementation, integration and delivery expertise that adapts those foundations to an organisation's environment.

Canonical HTML: https://kumuluz.com/en/company/about/

Additional English context: [Kumuluz platforms and accelerators](https://kumuluz.com/en/kumuluz/).

## Product foundations and tailored delivery

The current family contains [KumuluzAI Platform](kumuluz-ai.md), [Kumuluz API](kumuluz-api.md), [Kumuluz Business APIs](business-apis.md), [Kumuluz Digital Platform](digital-platform.md) and [Kumuluz Crowdsensing](crowdsensing.md).

The approach combines shared capabilities with project-specific engineering:

- Enterprise architecture across AI agents, APIs, services, workflows, integrations and data platforms.
- Implementation and integration with existing systems, identity providers, APIs and data sources.
- Cloud-native and DevOps delivery involving containers, Kubernetes/OpenShift, CI/CD, automation and observability.
- AgenticAI delivery covering agents, assistants, RAG, controlled tools, approvals and auditability.
- API and workflow delivery covering API ecosystems, reusable Business APIs and Temporal/Camunda implementation patterns.
- Evolution from a focused use case into broader platform adoption.

The distinction matters: product capabilities provide a reusable starting point, while configuration, interfaces, process semantics, security policies and operational responsibilities are determined for the delivery.

## Enterprise use and research context

Kumuluz products have been used across banking, insurance, aviation maintenance and enterprise services, including OTP banka, Generali, Prva zavarovalnica, Zavarovalniška skupina Sava and Adria Tehnika.

Kumuluz Crowdsensing has been used in the context of Green.Dat.AI, a Horizon Europe project focused on energy-efficient AI-ready data spaces and sustainable data analytics.

Typical application areas include governed enterprise agents, employee knowledge access, partner APIs, customer portals, reusable onboarding and payment capabilities, modernization and smart community data services. ERP, CRM, document and billing systems are typical integration targets.

## Cloud-native heritage and current direction

KumuluzEE is an earlier Sunesis microservice framework. It received the Java Duke's Choice Award at JavaOne in 2015.

The current Digital Platform direction is framework-flexible, MicroProfile/Jakarta EE-compatible and Quarkus-first for new Java cloud-native projects. KumuluzEE remains part of the historical story and is maintained where needed for existing environments, rather than serving as the primary foundation for new projects.

## Governance and engagement boundaries

Sunesis describes security, identity, controlled tool/API access, auditability, observability and human oversight as enterprise architecture concerns from the beginning. The customer environment determines the concrete policies, data sources, deployment model and approval requirements.

Licensing, support and service levels are agreed with Sunesis for each engagement.
